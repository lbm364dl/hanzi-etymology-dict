### First-run review and source-access improvement

The initial ten-character run finished with six entries published, three held for revision, and 电 failing before writing because the only selected Commons SVG returned HTTP 404. Independent review found concrete issues in the held entries: 的 reversed the traditional account's 旳→的 graphic direction; 第 assigned the Old Chinese reconstructions for 弟 and 第 to the wrong characters and mislabeled ⺮ as an uncertain ancestor instead of a side form of 竹; 动 omitted its current learner gloss and phrased a source limitation as an absolute fact. These findings are being repaired with fresh review rather than patched into approved prose.

Xiaoxuetang was referenced in eight of ten first-run research dossiers, but most records were rights notices or generic query pages: a GET page did not expose the actual forms. The first query-helper prototype discovered seven useful image records for 電 (including a late Western Zhou bronze form), but its eager queries for the first five batch-06 characters received HTTP 401. I changed it to query only after glyph research finds no usable image or a selected image fails acquisition; it prefers one locally recorded traditional counterpart and otherwise queries the entry form, spaces requests by at least two seconds, and pauses for an hour after HTTP 401. Results stay in the per-character run folder and go to a targeted research pass; only selected outputs, citations and reviewed snapshots can reach a published entry. This is not a database mirror.

The source adapter, its editorial integration, and the missing-glyph-citation retry are covered by the full pipeline suite: 73 tests passed on 2026-09-26. At the time of this snapshot, five review-driven repairs and five batch-06 characters were running concurrently; the remaining five batch-06 characters were queued to keep total character-job concurrency at ten.
