### Pipeline and source changes

Repeated use of Unihan readings for modern sound checks justified a small, licensed local extract rather than repeated manual lookup. `pipeline/data/unihan-kmandarin-hsk1.tsv` contains only the project-scoped `kMandarin` rows needed for the HSK cohort and pilot component forms; it includes Unicode's license notice. The pipeline prefers the full local Unihan file when available and falls back to the extract. Entries still cite character-specific Unicode records. A validator now checks a proposed Modern Mandarin pair against the local row only when that pair cites Unihan evidence, so a supported alternate reading without such a citation is not rejected automatically.

The other heavily cited dictionaries remain per-character links: their current reuse terms do not establish a blanket right to mirror the full datasets. The refreshed frequency snapshot in `RESEARCH.md` counts distinct HSK-list entries with evidence from each domain and explicitly does not treat repeated links as independent confirmation. Of 119 published pilot entries, 107 match the 300-character HSK cohort; 103 have reviewed v2 records, while 明、水、休、字 remain in an older format. Twelve entries are outside the HSK list.

This pass also clarified the component-form rule: an explicit source statement that a side form is positional supports `full_form`; a merely related alternate remains `variant_form`; `earlier_form` is reserved for a source-backed historical predecessor. Unicode mappings establish present-day form correspondence, not chronology.
