## Lessons incorporated

- Repairs receive fresh factual and readability reviews directly after their revision;
  the initial copy edit is not repeated after every revision. Repeated copy editing had
  undone exact transcriptions and expanded short learner explanations again.
- Every agent receives exact local Unihan principal-reading rows and Baxter–Sagart
  table rows for explicit entry/component/scoped-host forms in its input. The packet
  does not infer graphic or sound roles, and facts still require citable dossier records.
- Source gaps can trigger `refine --research-first`, preserving new evidence and
  binding subsequent approvals to the augmented dossier hash.
- Historical sound comparisons require aligned readings within the same reconstruction
  system. A mixed Baxter/Zhengzhang pair is not repaired by listing both system names.
  Already aligned pairs from multiple systems do not suffer that problem.
- The learner interface selects current reading comparisons and hides a historical
  component card only when the current card explicitly covers that form and role.
  The expert retains all scoped components, historical comparisons, and limitations.
- Reviewers must read `subject predicate object` before flagging a direction error:
  `拿 derived_from 拏` already expresses the later popular form deriving from 拏.
- Review the learner overview together with its cards and displayed sound pairs.
  Do not demand the same current readings and qualifications in every paragraph.
