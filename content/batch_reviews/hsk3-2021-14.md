## Lessons applied to fresh research

- 认/認 is a supported whole-character simplified/traditional correspondence. That
  alone does not establish that 人 historically replaced 忍. The current visible split
  can be discussed with an accurately unknown role where supported, while the sound
  analysis of 忍 must remain scoped to 認. Research should supply any claimed internal
  correspondence rather than infer it from the counterpart pair.
- 們 and 们 reading citations cannot substantiate 門 mén. The exact local Unihan row
  verifies the transcription but does not turn an inaccessible external page into an
  inspected source. The 们 follow-up researches an actual citable 門 reading record.
- Judge the complete learner layer, including its takeaway, before reporting an
  unexplained borrowing mechanism. 没's concise overview has a separate cited takeaway
  explaining proposed sign reuse for a different word because the words may have
  sounded alike, with the sound match explicitly unverified.
