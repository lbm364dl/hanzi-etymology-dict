## Changes learned from the pilot

- Separate the current learner split from historical scoped components in expert detail.
- Validate Japanese word examples, kana association, citation references and language.
- Interpret 本字, 旧字 and 教育用漢字 at the actual form described by the source.
- Retire incorrect source paraphrases while retaining their records for audit.
- Map citation IDs consistently in retirement metadata and review feedback.
- Remove the duplicate complete Chinese article from later Japanese review packets.
- Use targeted, optionally restricted JSON-path edits and bounded repair attempts.
- Validate complete patched articles, report schema errors together, and reject overlaps.
- Preserve model-generated prose and untouched expert/lexical fields during narrow repairs.
- Keep historical sound roles distinct from Japanese native readings.

## Remaining limits

The pilot required at least 688 completed retained Luna low invocations. Most were review
and revision calls. This is too costly for expansion: cumulative character budgets and a
better way to settle contradictory/nonactionable review requests remain necessary. Per-job
revision limits alone did not bound the repeated manual repair invocations.

The optional 学 clarification to separately identify its current roof component timed out
before fresh reviews. It did not replace the approved entry; its current learner card groups
the abbreviated upper shape, with ⺍ and 冖 named in expert detail. 聞 has no selected approved
glyph image. Local JMdict with spelling/reading restrictions would strengthen word-example
checks. Appending a component currently requires full-array edits, increasing payload size.
