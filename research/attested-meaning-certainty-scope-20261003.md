# Attested use and original-meaning priority

The 本 repair smoke repeatedly bundled evidence for an attested plant-root use with
an uncertain claim that this was the original meaning. Prior writer guidance could
be read as requiring sense certainty to be lowered whenever original-priority was
uncertain. This obscures a supported occurrence and passes the same ambiguity into
generated has_sense edges.

Writer, editor and independent-review instructions now distinguish evidence for
the attested use from priority of that use in character formation. A supported
occurrence keeps its appropriate certainty; an uncertain originality interpretation
belongs in separately cited qualified prose/limitations. The sense edge represents
that scoped use. Current-versus-historical scope still requires separate language-
specific evidence; neither certainty nor a dictionary listing invents current use.

A structural regression verifies an established historical use stays established
in the assembled edge while its separate original-priority account remains qualified.
No canonical entry or approval was edited by these prompt changes. Actual 本 revisions
and reviews remain retained; the final candidate still needs normal publication gates.
