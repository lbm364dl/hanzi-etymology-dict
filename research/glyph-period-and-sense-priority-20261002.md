# Glyph period and sense priority (2026-10-02)

Independent 大 reviews identified two recurring ambiguities: an inscription cited for an old meaning was made to imply earliest attestation without establishing priority, and a displayed modern redraw borrowed the date of a separately cited specimen without a verified image-to-specimen link.

Writer and review policy now explicitly distinguishes an attested example from demonstrated priority, requires current-use evidence for current senses, and gives redraw period fields a script-style/redraw description. Specimen provenance remains in citations unless the exact connection is verified. This applies to all characters and languages; it does not change published prose or certify old entries. 大 requires regenerated authorship and fresh independent reviews.
