# Learner card identity after component edits

The 茶 smoke candidate had current records ordered 艹,朩,人, while two learner cards used indices that pointed to the other shape. Numeric coverage passed and both independent reviews initially missed the semantic mismatch. The coordinator caught the mismatch before publication; those actual approvals remain retained, and an actual author is repairing the indices with fresh independent reviews.

Review packets now resolve every complete-array component index to its form, scope and linked learner-card text. Instructions require checking the described identity, not just numeric coverage. A contract test deliberately supplies swapped card texts and verifies that reviewers receive the true resolved targets without silently repairing the article. This generalizes to reordered historical/current records; it does not guess component identity by parsing Chinese prose or change existing approvals.
