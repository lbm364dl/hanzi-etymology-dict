# Separate local scan provenance from citation URLs

A genuine narrowly scoped 学 chronology research result used a local PNG path
as its `url`. The dossier validator rejected that external-evidence field; the
result remains preserved and a separate genuine replacement task supplies an
edition-matching bibliography URL. No coordinator rewrote the original result
or its hash to pretend it passed.

Research instructions now distinguish local inspected-scan provenance from the
external citation URL. A catalogue identifies the book edition, while the page
field and scan record establish what was inspected; the catalogue does not
support the etymological claim itself. This applies to future local books and
does not loosen external-evidence validation or recertify any article.
