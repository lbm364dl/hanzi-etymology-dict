# Interpret retained retired evidence as provenance

The related 学 source-paraphrase repair uses the existing `retired_evidence_ids` gate to preserve incorrect old records while preventing active citation. Because research, authors and reviewers receive the full provenance dossier, the retained old prose could still be mistaken for current claim support or for a current article assertion.

Shared writer/reviewer guidance now explicitly distinguishes archived retired records from active supporting evidence. Authors cite only current replacements that support the exact claim. Reviewers reject active retired citations, but must not demand deletion of immutable provenance or transfer a retired record's wrong claim into an otherwise correct article. This does not certify any replacement record: genuine scan research and exact new dossier/article reviews remain necessary.

The mechanical retired-citation gate is unchanged. This guidance accompanies the bounded 学 correction and applies to other sources without character conditionals. No new issue or published prose edit is introduced.
