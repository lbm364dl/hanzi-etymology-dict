# Honest resolution of absent citation metadata

The genuine 八 source-resolution reviewer read printed page 64 but used
`unresolved_identity_not_used` for the corpus's intentionally unextracted page-label
field. That category was false: this was a verified label, not an unidentified graph.
The executed result was retained and independently rejected before publication.

The general source-resolution contract now offers a separate, opt-in
`verified_metadata_not_extracted` disposition for absent printed-page metadata.
It requires an exact retained finding, corpus PDF page, absent current field value,
proposed printed label and source decoded-pixel hash. The original scan is independently
validated and prioritised among actual attachments. A genuine Luna low reviewer must
observe the matching label and confirm that the finding concerns citation metadata,
not a needed transcription or graph-identity repair. The result is bound to the
unchanged exact article/dossier/findings and real stage receipt.

The publication gate rechecks current corpus absence, source identity and original
pixels, exact metadata coverage and matching observed labels. It rejects a missing
check, changed or conflicting label, misclassified unused-identity disposition, wrong
field, or changed source pixels. It does not populate producer or consumer metadata,
claim an OCR repair, alter raw OCR or approve authored content.

Three focused regression checks passed: new metadata binding/invalidation cases,
existing exact-pair source-resolution binding, and existing literal-repair key scoping.
The previously rejected 八 receipt remains retained; a fresh real metadata check is
required before its candidate can be published. This is a general provenance distinction,
not a character-specific exception or a rule about glyph interpretation.
