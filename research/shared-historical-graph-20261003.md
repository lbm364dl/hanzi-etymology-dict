# Historical shared graphs

Reconciliation of 小 issue #92 exposed a schema gap: a qualified claim that two characters shared an earlier graph was encoded as variant_of because the available graphic predicates asserted variant identity or directional development. Qualified prose does not repair a stronger structured predicate.

The generic relationship schema now offers shares_historical_graph_with for distinct character endpoints. It expresses symmetric historical association, with its period, source-specific interpretation and certainty carried in the evidence-bound explanation. It does not establish contemporary interchangeability, derivation direction, or component continuity. Existing published entries are not automatically rewritten or certified. 小 requires genuine authorship and fresh independent reviews, including its separately identified duplicate small-sense records.
