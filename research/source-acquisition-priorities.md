# Source acquisition priorities

Updated: 2026-09-27. This is a provisional ranking by expected benefit to this project's editorial pipeline, not a frequency ranking of Outlier's complete bibliography. References in the opened 啚 entry and the later 学 / 學 inspection have been observed on the user's phone. No ongoing phone or Outlier access is required by the pipeline.

## 1. 季旭昇 — 說文新證

**Preferred edition:** 藝文印書館, September 2014 revised edition (第二版), one volume. The inspected Outlier bibliography cites 啚 at p. 462. That page has not been read directly.

**Expected benefit:** Direct character-by-character research for historical components, graphic changes, older meanings and competing analyses. This is the first acquisition to pursue for improving entries rather than relying on abbreviated intermediary accounts. Our source tree does not contain this book; published dossiers for 星 and 第 currently encounter its name through intermediary websites.

**Edition verification:** [Fudan publication announcement](https://fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/2369) confirms the 2014 revision and describes added analyses and glyphs. Earlier Taiwan volumes and the 2010 mainland edition are useful alternatives, but do not assume that their contents or page numbers match the 2014 edition.

**Small digitization pilot:** title/copyright page, index entry and complete 啚 discussion around p. 462, including references and any continuation page. Then a small selection of characters with disputed or changed components, before scanning the whole book.

## 2. 李學勤 主編 — 字源

**Edition to investigate:** 2012, three volumes; catalogue records include 天津古籍出版社. Confirm the imprint and edition from the actual copyright page before acquisition or indexing.

**Expected benefit:** A broad reference to complement the first book, especially for tracing historical forms and explanatory discussions across many characters. This is an additional research recommendation, not a reference observed in the opened Outlier entry. The word 字源 appears in many dossier field titles and queries; those occurrences do not establish citations to this particular book.

**Availability leads:** [Chinese Ancient Books academic catalogue](https://academic.ancientbooks.cn/docLunzhu/lunzhuPeriodView.jspx?id=1468578); [university library description](https://library.xujc.com/_upload/article/files/b7/0e/0e73e9df4be187b0c82f8fd8751d/72bbeba8-2ed6-4f04-9c09-fab4fbfb004b.pdf). A [USC-hosted reference PDF](https://scalar.usc.edu/works/chinese-studies-reference-collection/media/PL1281%20.Z53%202012%20%E5%AD%97%E6%BA%90_OCR.pdf) also surfaced in search, but its coverage, edition and access/reuse status have not been evaluated. Check existing institutional access before spending effort on a new scan.

## 3. 裘錫圭 — 文字學概要

**For general research:** 商務印書館, 2013 修訂本. **For exact reference tracing:** the inspected Outlier bibliography names a 萬卷樓 edition, describes a February 2006 eighth printing, and cites p. 156. Its displayed 1991 edition label still needs reconciliation with catalogue metadata. Do not equate p. 156 across editions.

**Expected benefit:** Research methodology and consistent distinctions between formation, graphic change, borrowing, and character/word history. It has less direct character-by-character coverage value than the two references above, but can improve instructions and verification criteria.

**Edition verification:** [Fudan publication announcement and author preface](https://www.fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/2100) documents the 2013 revision. [CiNii catalogue](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB26206647) records a 1995 萬卷樓 edition; it does not verify the exact printing shown on the phone.

## Digitization format that will help the pipeline

- Keep readable page images alongside OCR. Historical glyphs must survive as images; ordinary OCR will often misread them.
- Capture title, copyright/edition page, contents, character index and bibliography.
- Preserve printed page numbers separately from PDF page indices, and the volume identifier.
- Start with a small pilot at roughly 400 dpi; check tiny glyphs and footnotes before choosing settings for the rest.
- Include complete entries, continuation pages, figure labels and references rather than isolated paragraphs.
- We can later index by character and component and expose exact page ranges to research agents. A searchable PDF with original page images is already a useful starting artifact.

## How priorities should evolve

Record additional references while the phone is available, including the character, exact edition/page and access gap. Promote specialist books or papers when several concrete unresolved entries need them. Avoid acquiring large glyph corpora first: the project already has several online historical-form sources, while direct analytical scholarship is the more immediate gap.

## Additional acquisitions (2026-09-27)

These are independently researched recommendations, not references inspected in the user's Outlier entry. Their priority follows the three books above.

### 4. 劉釗 — 古文字構形學（修訂本）

Prefer the 2011 revised edition, 福建人民出版社. Most directly relevant additional analytical source: component construction, graphic transformation, ornamental strokes and changes that affect interpretation of sound components. Useful for expert explanations and research/review instructions. [Fudan announcement and author prefaces](https://www.fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/1511) describe the revision and research scope.

### 5. 漢語大字典 — 第二版, complete nine-volume edition (2010)

四川辭書出版社 / 崇文書局. A character-level reference for readings, senses and historical examples. Expected benefit is checking meaning-history claims and distinguishing documented uses from an inferred evolution. A dictionary sense ordering alone is not a chronology or proof of a semantic transition. [Sichuan University project](https://hydzd.scu.edu.cn/) and [edition catalogue](https://search.worldcat.org/zh-tw/title/%3A-Hanyu-da-zidian/oclc/636381185). Prefer the complete edition rather than an abridgment for research.

### 6. 董蓮池 — 新金文編 (2011, three volumes)

作家出版社. Lower priority than the analytical books, but useful for exact bronze glyph forms and source attribution. [Fudan publication announcement](https://www.fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/1749). Acquisition improves research access; it does not itself grant permission to publish reproduced glyph images. Keep original source identifiers with each selected form.

### Optional: 漢語大詞典

For word-level historical uses and compounds, complementing the character-focused 漢語大字典. Lower immediate priority because our entries are character-based. [Official dictionary service](https://app.hanyudacidian.cn/) describes its coverage. Existing digital access may be more practical than digitizing a large printed set.

## Further specialist references

These are additional research recommendations, not newly inspected Outlier bibliography items. Ranked by expected marginal benefit for the project, with the first two particularly useful.

### 7. 古文字詁林（修訂本）, twelve volumes

古文字詁林編纂委員會, 上海教育出版社. A collected reference for tracing scholarly interpretations and disagreements; researchers should still follow the underlying studies and check their dates. [Revised-edition catalogue](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BC07623994), set ISBN 9787544487795. Keep the index with the volumes. Do not confuse this with 丁福保's 說文解字詁林, a different collection.

### 8. 蒋绍愚 — 汉语历史词汇学概要

商务印书馆. The 2015 standalone book is an option; the publisher also lists a 2024 edition as 蒋绍愚文集（第二卷）, ISBN 9787100232586. Useful for building meaning-history methodology and distinguishing changes in words and senses from changes in writing. It is a methodological complement, not a per-character etymology dictionary. [Publisher description and contents](https://www.cp.com.cn/book/ba8053bc-7.html).

### 9. 刘钊主编 — 新甲骨文编（增订本）

福建人民出版社, 2014 enlarged edition. Oracle-bone forms and their references, complementing 新金文编. Original edition documented by [Fudan](https://fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/830); the enlarged book is discussed in an [ECNU academic lecture announcement](https://rwsk.ecnu.edu.cn/ec/ae/c25014a257198/page.htm), and its 2014 bibliography is recorded in [Chinese Studies](https://ccs.ncl.edu.tw/files/current_chinese_studies/01_041_001_01_01.pdf). Preserve original glyphs and source identifiers; OCR text alone is insufficient.

### 10. 汤余惠主编 — 战国文字编（修订本）

福建人民出版社, 2015. Fills the Warring States stage between earlier inscriptions and later standardized writing; especially helpful when an explanation depends on regional forms. [Chinese Encyclopedia entry](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?ID=524722&SiteID=1&SubID=44733&Type=bkzyb) verifies the 2001 original and 2015 revision. Acquisition is a research aid, not automatic permission to reproduce its figures.
