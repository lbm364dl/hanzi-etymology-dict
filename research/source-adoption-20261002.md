# Counting existing approved source research

The source queue previously counted only publications made through its own job path.
This missed independently reviewed entries that already used the registered book.
Regenerating their prose solely to create a queue receipt would obscure provenance.

The adoption harness preserves exact canonical article/dossier hashes and original
factual/readability approvals. A separate actual Luna low source-coverage agent checks
used page-specific book evidence against attached original scans. Its saved result and
metadata are checked again when counting completion. An unused book record in a dossier
does not qualify. This is a general source accounting rule, not a character exception.

The contrasting smoke cases exposed that distinction: 半 passed the actual source check;
教 was refused because none of its book evidence IDs are cited by the approved article.
学 has used book evidence and was submitted for a separate check. Refusal does not erase
the research or imply the explanation is false; further enrichment remains required.

Fourteen source-enrichment/adoption tests passed, including unchanged canonical bytes,
retained original approvals, refusal of unused book evidence, and invalidation when the
saved coverage verdict is altered. No new authorship approvals were fabricated.
