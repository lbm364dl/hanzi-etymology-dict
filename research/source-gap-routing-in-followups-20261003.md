# Source gaps in independent research follow-ups

The narrowly scoped 学 source-unit research correctly retained an unresolved
printed identity in its genuine `gaps` output. However, the direct research
invocation did not receive the source-enrichment harness's marker instructions.
The collector recognizes explicit scan/OCR markers, so an unmarked identity gap
could be missed when those research receipts are later handed to publication.

The shared research instructions now require the same exact marker prefixes as
the source-enrichment policy. This applies to independent follow-ups and future
books. A scan identity gap and a proposed OCR replacement remain different
findings; neither establishes an applied producer correction.

This is prompt guidance, not a semantic classifier or retrospective approval.
The collector's behavior and publication gates remain unchanged. Existing real
outputs must remain intact. Known older unmarked gaps must be explicitly carried
as coordinator findings with their actual research provenance, followed by a
genuine source-resolution review bound to the final article and dossier. 学 still
awaits those publication gates.
