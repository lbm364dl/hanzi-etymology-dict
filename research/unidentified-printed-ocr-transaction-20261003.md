# Unidentified printed graph transactions

A scan can disprove the raw OCR scalar without establishing an exact Unicode
replacement. Generic unresolved_identity only means the comparison remains undecided;
it must not authorize a source edit. The new unsupported_raw_identity verdict requires
null printed_text and an actual independently bound occurrence receipt explicitly
finding the raw scalar unsupported by the pixels. Confirmed literal replacements
remain a separate verdict. Ordinary correct_raw and generic unresolved_identity
receipts cannot authorize marker substitution.

The producer transaction can now preserve such a graph as its native page-bound glyph
reference with an unidentified_printed_character record and explicit uncertainty.
It preserves original OCR, copies and hashes the actual review attachments, serializes
producer/corpus writes, rebuilds and verifies consumer text/glyph records, and rolls
back shared exports on failure. No Unicode identity is guessed from the quotation.
