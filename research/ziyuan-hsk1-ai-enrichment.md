The entry calls 愛 a phonosemantic character, analyzes it as 从夂、㤅聲, and says 愛 developed from 㤅. It separately quotes the received *Shuowen* accounts for 愛 and 㤅, cites the Guodian 《老子》甲 slip wording 甚㤅(爱)必大費 and a Zhongshan inscription, and says Han sources commonly use 愛. The page does not prove one universal lineage or date the transfer to the affection sense. In particular, keep this account's 㤅 phonetic proposal distinct from the disagreement over the upper element *inside* 㤅.

Other inspected research distinguishes analyses of early 㤅: 字源 and some dictionary traditions identify 旡, while CUHK identifies 兂 as the phonetic element. 游國慶 discusses 旡/既 forms and regional histories in a separate analysis. Preserve those as attributed alternatives. No matched same-system ancient reading pair for 兂/旡 and 㤅 was verified. Do not infer a present-day function for the 友-shaped part of simplified 爱 from either ancient analysis.
