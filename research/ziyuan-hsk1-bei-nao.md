## 備 (HSK 1 备)

- **Location:** PDF page 718; printed page **705**, visible in the bottom margin. Corpus evidence SHA-256 `b5a8b57db4cc9b093ced2a56273ee7a1bfa84a59f713626a2973aa21696c0cc9`; scan `research-ocr/ziyuan-full/page-0718/source.png`.
- The headword is **備(备) bèi**, with a sequence of seven labeled forms: Western Zhou, Warring States, *Shuowen* ancient form, *Shuowen* small seal, two Han forms, traditional regular script, and simplified regular script. The book cites 《甲金篆》545 and 《说文》163 for these forms.
- The author calls it a phonosemantic character with original meaning “cautious; guard against,” quotes *Shuowen* “備，慎也。从人，[printed phonetic glyph]声,” and argues the initial phonetic component was the original graph for 箙 (a quiver/container for arrows). The text describes it as arrows stored in a container; says it was already corrupted in Western Zhou but still traceable, became unrecognizable by Warring States, was regularized to a different graph in small seal, and later changed again in traditional regular script. 人 remained stable. In simplified script, the author says a cursive form of the phonetic was selected and the whole graph fixed as 备, losing visible phonosemantic structure. Signed 董莲池. These are the book author's assertions, summarized here without assigning Unicode readings to the rare forms.
- **OCR check:** The OCR substitutes the common character 葡 for the book's unusual printed glyph at the *Shuowen* quotation and in the form-change account. At PDF p.718 / printed p.705, the scan visibly shows the same uncommon printed graph in the *Shuowen* small-seal slot and in the explanatory prose; it is not safely identifiable as 葡 from these pixels. The character sequence around it (箙;盛矢器; arrows stored inside; later graph change) is legible, but the rare glyph identity remains unresolved. The OCR also flattens the form sequence. This is a consequential character-recognition error, not a cosmetic issue.
- **Effect on approved entry** (`content/entries/5907.json`): substantively corroborates the existing account of the person + arrow-container/quiver interpretation, phonetic-role uncertainty, and simplification destroying visible structure. It adds a precise author-attributed developmental chain and references to the graph tables, but the current summary already covers the material point. No correction to approved prose is justified from this source alone; consider retaining the detailed transition as source evidence in a future dossier refresh, after exact rare form verification.
